Consigliere Records

Consigliere Records is the underworld’s secret handshake—a label built on grit, loyalty, and the art of making street tales immortal. Founded in the shadows and moving like a syndicate, it’s not here for the fame, the clout, or the mainstream’s approval. It’s for those who understand the language of corner politics, survival, and the cost of staying true.

At the forefront is Ant Kelly, a hustler in and out of the booth, spitting raw narratives soaked in the fumes of graffiti runs, psychedelic revelations, and the restless mind of a misfit. His bars move through the forgotten corners of the city—**abandoned houses, the flickering lights of corner stores, backroom deals, and the coded language of the drug trade**—balancing the weight of the streets with moments of rebellion and self-discovery. But Ant is more than just a storyteller. His passion for creating extends beyond music; he’s also an artist, a graphic designer, and a fashion designer, bringing his visions to life through digital art, drawings, and clothes. His creative drive knows no bounds, and he pours that same energy into every aspect of his work, merging music, art, and fashion into one cohesive movement. But he isn’t just streetwise; his music dives deeper, laced with conscious lyricism and powerful, thought-provoking tracks that challenge the system and uplift those trapped within it.

Consigliere isn’t boxed in by tradition. While its foundation is built on grimy, dust-coated boom bap, its architects aren’t afraid to break boundaries. They also experiment with other sounds and genres, such as indie, punk rock, electronic, and glitchcore—pulling from anything that resonates with the underground spirit. Ant’s artistic influence seeps into every project, where visual aesthetics blend seamlessly with his sonic creations, and the result is something raw, unique, and relentlessly creative.

DJ Krooked is the mastermind behind the sonics, weaving hypnotic loops that bend reality, laced with psychedelic textures that feel like a bad trip in the best way. His production moves like a dream caught between nostalgia and paranoia, effortlessly blending raw boom bap with eerie atmospheres and unexpected genre shifts. Like Ant, Krooked is also a passionate creator in multiple mediums—drawing, graphic design, and digital art. His work extends beyond just sound; it’s a visual and artistic journey that reflects his experimental nature. He’s an artist in every sense of the word, and his unique designs add another layer of depth to the Consigliere aesthetic.

DB lurks in the cut, a silent enforcer with verses sharp enough to leave a mark. His punchlines land like gut punches, coded for those who really live it—poetry laced with danger, wisdom, and the kind of wit that only comes from surviving the trenches.

Consigliere Records doesn’t play by industry rules. No social media gimmicks, no trend-chasing. Just Bandcamp, Spotify, and an underground presence that refuses to be diluted. Their work is found in the hands of collectors, passed around like contraband, living on **Paka The Plug, Still Villain, DITCD**, and whispers between those who know. This is more than a label—it’s a code.
